    How this works

    Once a day a script collects the last 24 hours of world news from wire services and international outlets. Five AI experts each read it from their own angle: nuclear, climate, biological risk, AI and disruptive technology, and geopolitics. Each one scores the day and must point to real headlines.

    A sixth AI, the chair, weighs their views against yesterday’s reading and sets the clock. It can move at most five seconds a day, and holding still is always allowed. The board starts from the official 2026 setting of 85 seconds.

    This is a simulation. The real Doomsday Clock is set once a year by the Bulletin of the Atomic Scientists’ Science and Security Board, not by software. Treat this page as a daily mood reading, not a forecast.
