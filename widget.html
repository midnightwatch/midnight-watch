The one line

Paste this where you want the clock. Anywhere that accepts HTML works: WordPress (Custom HTML block), Ghost, Substack pages, Notion (Embed), Squarespace, Wix, or your own site.

<iframe src="https://midnightwatch.github.io/midnight-watch/embed.html" width="360" height="130" style="border:0;border-radius:8px;max-width:100%" title="Midnight Watch: a daily AI Doomsday Clock" loading="lazy"></iframe>

Options

  • Size: change width and height. A wide box puts the clock beside the text; a tall box stacks them.
  • Light background: use embed.html?theme=light.
  • Clicking it opens Midnight Watch in a new tab.

What it does and doesn’t do

It loads one small page from this site and reads today’s number. Nothing else.

No cookies, no tracking of your visitors, no ads, nothing to sign up for, nothing to pay. Remove it any time by deleting the line.

Please keep the link back; that’s the only thing asked in return.
